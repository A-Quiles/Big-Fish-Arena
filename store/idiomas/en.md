## App name
Big Fish Arena

## Short description (max. 80)
Eat smaller fish, grow bigger and don't get eaten. A brand-new sea every run!

## Full description (max. 4000)
You start out as a tiny fish in a sea full of danger. Gobble up the little ones, grow size by size and flee from anything that can swallow you whole. How far can you go?

🐟 12 SPECIES WITH UNIQUE ABILITIES
Sardine, clownfish, pufferfish, flying fish, piranha, octopus, barracuda, stingray, swordfish, electric eel, anglerfish and shark. Each one plays differently: speed dashes, ink clouds, electric shocks, poison, ramming charges, glowing lures…

🌊 A DIFFERENT SEA EVERY RUN
The map is randomly generated and has no edges: keep swimming in one direction and you'll go all the way around the sea. Coral reef, kelp forest, lagoon, open sea, abyss and abyssal trench: every zone has its own fish and its own level of danger. Just like the real ocean, each species lives at its own depth: flying fish just below the surface, octopuses, stingrays and crabs hugging the seabed, and the anglerfish down in the darkest depths.

❄️☢️ THREE SEAS
Besides the Temperate Sea, you can unlock Antarctica, with ice floes, igloos, penguins, orcas and polar bears that dive in to hunt you (land on the ice and you'd better get back in the water fast!), and the Toxic Sea, where plastic makes you shrink, pipes pour out poison and radioactive barrels burn.

🦈 BITES AND HEALTH
Every fish has health, and every bite counts. If you're almost twice as big, you swallow your prey in one gulp. Colored outlines tell you at a glance who's prey and who's a threat.

🐟🐟🐟 NEW MODE: SCHOOL OF FISH
Reach size 6 to unlock a whole new way to play: instead of one fish, you control an entire school of sardines. Gather krill and stray sardines to grow up to 50 strong, attack much bigger fish all at once, and pack into a spinning bait ball when a predator lunges at your school.

🎣 WATCH OUT FOR FISHERMEN
If a boat shows up flying the diving flag, there are divers with spearguns nearby. They aim at you with a red line: swim up or down to dodge the shot… and if you're brave enough, bite them all the way back to their boat.

🎚️ CHOOSE YOUR DIFFICULTY
Easy, Normal, Hard or Custom: tune predator aggression, giant fish, damage, speed and fishermen. The harder it gets, the more pearls you earn: up to ×3.

🐬 LEAP OUT OF THE WATER
Swim fast toward the surface to jump, hunt seagulls and snap up insects, or crash down on other fish with an epic belly flop.

🏆 ALWAYS SOMETHING TO GO FOR
Earn pearls, unlock species, open chests full of skins, upgrade every fish, use boosters and complete missions (hard missions too!). Fill in the species guide to discover them all.

🎨 FISH WITH TONS OF PERSONALITY
They eye their prey, panic when a predator shows up, look blissfully happy when they swallow and get dizzy from electric shocks. A sea with different light, layers and colors in every zone.

No levels: every run is different. Play in landscape or portrait, with touch controls designed for mobile.
